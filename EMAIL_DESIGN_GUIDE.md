# OPSlyHR Email Design System

## Brand Identity Guidelines

**Tone:** Professional, polished, warm, trustworthy, clear and direct, human but not robotic

**Voice:** Confident and structured, supportive and helpful, focused on clarity and action

**Design System:** Clean, minimal, enterprise-grade with soft spacing and visual hierarchy

## DESIGN SPECIFICATIONS

### Color Palette:
- **Primary Brand Color:** #1E40AF (Professional Blue)
- **Accent Color:** #059669 (Trust Green - CTAs)
- **Background:** #FFFFFF (White)
- **Secondary Background:** #F9FAFB (Light Grey)
- **Text Primary:** #111827 (Dark Grey/Black)
- **Text Secondary:** #6B7280 (Medium Grey)
- **Border/Divider:** #E5E7EB (Subtle Grey)

### Typography:
- **Logo/Header:** 24px, Bold, Primary Color
- **Subject Line (in preview):** 18px, Bold, Dark Grey
- **Main Heading:** 20px, Semi-bold, Dark Grey
- **Body Text:** 16px, Regular, Medium Grey
- **Supporting Text:** 14px, Regular, Light Grey
- **CTA Button:** 16px, Semi-bold, White text on Accent Green background

### Spacing:
- Header padding: 40px top/bottom, 30px sides
- Section padding: 30px top/bottom, 25px sides
- Line height: 1.6 for body text
- Margin between sections: 20px
- Button padding: 12px vertical, 24px horizontal
- Card border-radius: 6px

### CTA Button Style:
- Background: #059669 (Accent Green)
- Text: White, semi-bold, 16px
- Padding: 12px 24px
- Border-radius: 6px
- Hover State: Darken to #047857
- No underline or shadow in inactive state
- Subtle shadow on hover

### Image/Logo:
- OPSlyHR logo should appear at top of every email
- Keep logo simple and clean
- Recommended size: 150px wide
- Centered in header area

### Mobile Optimization:
- Stack layout at 480px and below
- Full-width CTA buttons on mobile
- Larger touch targets (48px minimum height for buttons)
- Reduce padding on mobile (20px sides instead of 30px)
- Single-column layout for all templates

### Footer:
- Consistent across all emails
- Company name, contact email, copyright
- Very light grey background (#F9FAFB)
- 12px text, light grey color
- Centered alignment
- 20px padding top/bottom

## SENDING BEST PRACTICES

- **From:** noreply@opslyhr.com or specific team (success@, support@, vetting@)
- **Time:** Send during business hours in recipient's timezone when possible
- **Frequency:** One email per event—no marketing fluff
- **Link Handling:** All critical links should go to authenticated dashboard or secure token pages
- **Personalization:** Always use first name when available; never use generic "Hi there"
- **Test:** Always test mobile rendering before sending

---

This email design system ensures OPSlyHR communications feel premium, trustworthy, and consistent while maintaining professional clarity across all customer touchpoints.
